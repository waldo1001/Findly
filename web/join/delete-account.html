Delete your Findly account

Permanently delete your account and its data — no app reinstall needed.

This is permanent

Deleting your account happens immediately and cannot be undone. There is no recovery window and no way for support to reverse it. This deletes:

  • Your profile, phone-number sign-in, and registered devices
  • Your full location history and geofence ("Home"/"School" etc.) event history
  • Groups you own (deleted entirely); groups you've joined (you're removed from them)
  • Your usage records

If you are the only parent in your family, this also permanently deletes your entire family — including every other member's location history — for everyone. This page has no way to check that in advance, so please read this carefully either way.

To prove this is really your account, you'll first sign in the same way you do in the app: with your phone number and an SMS code.

Confirm deletion

This will immediately and permanently delete your account, devices, location history, geofence events, and owned groups — and, if you are the family's only parent, the whole family's data too. There is no undo.
