Who runs this

Findly is a private, family-oriented location-sharing app operated by [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY]. If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact [CONTACT EMAIL].

What we collect

  • Phone number — used only to sign you in (there are no passwords). Your phone number is handled by Firebase Authentication (operated by Google) and is not sent to or stored by our own backend, ever.
  • Location — latitude/longitude, accuracy, and the time each position was recorded, reported periodically by devices you register.
  • Device information — a device name/model, platform (Android/iOS), and a push notification token, so the app can wake your device and send you alerts.
  • Geofence events — if you set up a "place" (like Home or School), we record when a device enters or leaves it.
  • Group membership — if you create or join a temporary group, we store your display name for that group and your membership record for as long as the group exists.

We do not collect contacts, photos, messages, browsing history, or anything not listed above.

Who can see your data

Your location is visible only to the other members of your family (people you've explicitly invited) and, if you choose to join one, the other members of any temporary group you're part of (people who have your group's join code). We never sell your data, and we don't use it for advertising — there are no ad or analytics SDKs in this app.

Temporary groups are deliberately more limited: other members see only your current position and display name — never your device details, battery level, or location history.

How long we keep it

  • Family location history is kept for up to 400 days, though the app itself only lets you look back 90 days by default.
  • Temporary group locations are deleted automatically, typically within about a day of the group ending — the exact timing depends on which of three expiry options the group's creator chose (immediate deletion, a short grace period, or keeping just the member list as a memento with locations still deleted).
  • If you leave a family or a group, your future location is no longer shared with it, but historical records already stored follow the retention rules above.

Where your data is stored

Data is stored on Microsoft Azure infrastructure in the West Europe region. Authentication is handled by Firebase (Google). Push notifications are delivered via Firebase Cloud Messaging and, for iOS, Apple's Push Notification service.

Security

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Sign-in uses phone-number verification instead of passwords, removing an entire category of password-related risk. Temporary group data is deliberately minimized (position-only, see above) specifically to limit what could ever be exposed even in the event of a problem.
