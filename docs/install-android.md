# Install Findly on Android

Findly isn't on the Play Store yet, so it installs from a file instead. It takes about two minutes.

Your phone will show a couple of warnings along the way. That's normal for any app that doesn't come from the Play Store — steps 3 and 4 tell you exactly what you'll see and what to tap.

> **Open this page on the Android phone you want Findly on.** Downloading it on a computer won't help.

## ⬇️ [Download Findly](https://github.com/waldo1001/Findly/releases/download/android-v1.0.0-build6/Findly-1.0.0-build6.apk)

Android · 12 MB · version 1.0.0 (build 6)

---

### 1. Tap the download link above

Your browser will ask whether to keep a file called `Findly-1.0.0-build6.apk`.

Choose **Download** or **Keep**. Chrome warns about every file of this type, not this one in particular.

### 2. Open it

Tap the downloaded file. You'll usually find it in your notifications, or in your **Files** app under **Downloads**.

### 3. Allow this one app to install it

Android will say something like:

> *"For your security, your phone isn't allowed to install unknown apps from this source."*

Tap **Settings**, turn on **Allow from this source**, then press back.

You're granting this to whichever app you opened the file from — your browser — not to your whole phone.

### 4. Choose "Install anyway" when Play Protect asks

You may see **"Unsafe app blocked"**, or an offer to scan the app first.

This appears for anything that didn't come from the Play Store, whoever made it. Tap **Install anyway**, or **More details → Install anyway**.

### 5. Sign in with your phone number

Open Findly and enter your mobile number. You'll get a text message with a six-digit code — type that in and you're through.

### 6. Allow location

Findly asks for location so the family map can show where everyone is.

Choose **While using the app** to start. You can change it later in your phone's settings.

---

## Your number needs to be Belgian or Dutch

Right now Findly can only send the sign-in code to **+32** (Belgium) and **+31** (Netherlands) numbers.

If your number is from another country, nothing happens when you ask for the code — no error message, just no text. Let me know and I'll add your country.

## One thing for later

When Findly reaches the Play Store, you'll need to **uninstall this version first**, then install the Play one. Android won't replace a file-installed app with a Store one.

Nothing you've shared is lost — it lives on the server, not on your phone.
