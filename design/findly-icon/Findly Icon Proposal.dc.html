The Stepped F.

A soft location pin holding three shortening bars. Read one way it is the letter F; read another it is a signal resolving — a device answering back. The dashed halo around it is the quiet “we’re listening” ring.

Everything below is production-ready: exact geometry on a 1024 canvas, every iOS and Android variant, degradation rules, clear space, misuse, and the export manifest.

Rules

Clear space
Keep clear space of ¼ of the icon width on all sides in marketing and store listings. Inside the OS, the platform owns spacing — never add your own padding to the exported master.
