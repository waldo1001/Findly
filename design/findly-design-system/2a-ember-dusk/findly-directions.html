Botanical and airy; borderless surfaces, editorial type, generous air.

Deep leaf green against warm paper — the one hue family a map already contains, so chrome and tiles agree instead of fighting. Nothing is boxed: rows are separated by air and a single hairline, cards have no fill, and the sheet is a plain sheet of paper. Type carries the hierarchy instead, at lighter weights and larger sizes than the other three, with clay #A3541F for the secondary so accents read as ceramic rather than as a second brand. Axes moved: hue & saturation, surface strategy, type personality, density & rhythm.

1d

Signal

Near-monochrome ink with one electric accent; dense, geometric, high-contrast.

Ink is the brand: primary is #101010 on light and the accent is a single electric chartreuse that only ever appears on ink, at 17:1. The basemap is desaturated to near-grey so the one bright thing on screen is always the person you are looking for. Radii drop to 2–8px, rows compress to 56pt, and the last-seen ages are set as large tabular numerals — the type moment is a number, not a headline. This is the direction that survives direct sunlight best and the one that is fully shippable as a token swap. Axes moved: hue & saturation, shape language, type personality, density & rhythm.

Palette rule that must ship with it: secondary #E9FF3D is never text or an icon on a light surface (1.2:1). It is legal only as fill on primary, or as a fill behind ink. Enforce it in review — it is the one way this direction breaks.
